Past Adventures

Kể về những gì đã xảy ra — Past Simple (Quá khứ đơn) với động từ đều và bất quy tắc!

📖 Past Simple ✅ Regular & Irregular Verbs ❓ Did you…? 🚫 didn't + V
📚 Động Từ Bất Quy Tắc Quan Trọng (nhấn xem dạng quá khứ)
📐 Ngữ Pháp — Past Simple

✏️ 3 cấu trúc cần nhớ

Khẳng địnhS + V-ed / V2→ I visited Hội An. / She went home.
Phủ địnhS + didn't + V (nguyên thể)→ I didn't go to Huế. ✗ didn't went
Câu hỏiDid + S + V (nguyên thể)?→ Did you enjoy it? — Yes, I did.
✅ Regular verbs — thêm -ed:
visit → visited
cook → cooked
watch → watched
stop → stopped (gấp đôi cuối)
⚡ Signal words:
yesterday · last night
last week / month / year
in 2020 · two days ago
when I was young
💬 Mẫu Câu
"What did you do last weekend?"
→ Cuối tuần trước bạn đã làm gì?
💡 Câu hỏi quá khứ phổ biến nhất — học thuộc lòng!
"I went to the beach. We had a great time!"
→ Tôi đã đi biển. Chúng tôi đã có khoảng thời gian tuyệt vời!
💡 go → went · have → had · buy → bought (bất quy tắc phổ biến nhất)
"Did you enjoy the food?" — "Yes, it was delicious!" / "No, it wasn't very good."
→ Bạn có thích đồ ăn không? — Có, ngon lắm! / Không, không ngon lắm.
💡 was / were = dạng quá khứ của am/is/are
"I didn't sleep well last night because I was too excited."
→ Tôi đã không ngủ ngon tối qua vì tôi quá hào hứng.
💡 didn't + V nguyên thể (KHÔNG V-ed): ✗ "didn't went" → ✓ "didn't go"
📝 Câu Chuyện Mẫu — Kỳ Nghỉ Cuối Tuần

📖 Đọc và chú ý các động từ quá khứ (in đậm màu vàng)

Last Saturday, my family went to Vũng Tàu beach. We woke up early and took a bus at 7 a.m. When we arrived, the weather was perfect — sunny and warm.

We swam in the sea, ate fresh seafood, and walked along the beach. My sister bought some souvenirs. I took many photos and posted them online.

We didn't stay overnight — we came back home in the evening. I was tired but very happy. It was a wonderful day!
🗣️ Hội Thoại
A
Hey! How was your weekend?
B
It was amazing! I went to Đà Lạt with my friends.
A
Really? What did you do there?
B
We visited a flower garden, ate lots of local food, and stayed at a cosy guesthouse. The weather was perfect!
A
That sounds great! Did you take many photos?
B
Yes, I took hundreds! But I forgot my charger so my phone died on day two. I didn't take any photos after that!
A
Oh no! But it sounds like you had an amazing trip anyway!
✏️ Luyện Tập — Chia Động Từ Quá Khứ
Điền dạng đúng của động từ trong ngoặc:
1. Yesterday, I ___ (go) to the market.
2. She ___ (eat) phở for breakfast.
3. They ___ (not watch) TV last night.
4. ___ you ___ (enjoy) the party? (Yes/No Q)
5. The weather ___ (be) sunny and warm.
