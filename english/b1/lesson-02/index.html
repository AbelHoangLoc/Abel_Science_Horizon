Telling Stories

Kể chuyện sinh động bằng 3 thì quá khứ phối hợp — Past Perfect, Past Simple & Past Continuous!

📖 Past Perfect (had done) 🔄 3 thì quá khứ phối hợp 🔗 Connectors kể chuyện ✍️ Kỹ năng narrative
📐 Ba Thì Quá Khứ — Phối Hợp Kể Chuyện
Past Perfect
had + V3
Hành động xảy ra TRƯỚC một hành động khác trong quá khứ

"By the time she arrived, I had already left."
Past Simple
V-ed / V2
Hành động chính trong câu chuyện — xảy ra theo trình tự

"She walked in and saw the mess."
Past Continuous
was/were + V-ing
Bối cảnh, hành động ĐANG diễn ra khi sự việc xảy ra

"It was raining when I left."

✏️ Cấu trúc Past Perfect

Khẳng địnhS + had + V3→She had finished work before I called.
Phủ địnhS + hadn't + V3→I hadn't eaten anything all day.
Câu hỏiHad + S + V3?→Had you ever been there before?
💡 Từ kết hợp thường gặp: by the time · after · before · when · as soon as · because · already (had already + V3) · just (had just + V3)
🔗 Connectors — Kết Nối Câu Chuyện Mạch Lạc
📝 Câu Chuyện Mẫu — Đọc & Phân Tích Màu Sắc

🎨 Màu vàng = Past Perfect · Màu tím = Past Simple · Màu xanh = Past Continuous

It was a Thursday afternoon. Outside, it was raining heavily and the streets were almost empty. Lan sat at her desk, staring at the letter she had received that morning.

She had applied for the scholarship six months earlier and had almost given up hope. But now, the letter in her hands told a different story. She had won.

Suddenly, her phone rang. It was her mother. "Have you heard the news?" she asked. Lan laughed and wiped a tear from her eye. "Yes, Mum," she said. "I had been waiting for this moment my whole life."
💬 Mẫu Câu Kể Chuyện
"By the time I arrived at the cinema, the film had already started."
→ Khi tôi đến rạp, bộ phim đã bắt đầu rồi.
💡 by the time + Past Simple → sự kiện muộn hơn. had already + V3 → xảy ra trước.
"She was walking home when she suddenly heard a strange noise."
→ Cô ấy đang đi bộ về nhà thì đột nhiên nghe tiếng động lạ.
💡 was/were + V-ing (bối cảnh) + when + Past Simple (sự kiện bất ngờ)
"He had never seen anything like it before in his entire life."
→ Anh ấy chưa bao giờ thấy điều gì như vậy trong cả cuộc đời.
💡 had never + V3 = chưa bao giờ làm gì trước thời điểm đó
✏️ Luyện Tập — Điền Past Perfect Đúng

2. I was tired because I ___ (not sleep) well.
3. By the time he ___ (arrive), we ___ (eat) dinner./
4. She ___ (never travel) abroad before her trip to Japan.
